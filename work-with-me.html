Questions

Before you book

Who is flyflowhealing for?

Anyone, of any age or background, who feels depleted, stuck or far from themselves, and who wants healing that includes their faith, their relationships and their everyday habits.

Do I need to share a particular faith?

No. The work is openly faith-filled and speaks of Heaven’s power, but it is not tied to any one religion. You are welcome as you are.

Is this therapy or medical treatment?

No. flyflowhealing offers coaching and mentoring. It is not a substitute for medical or mental health care, and it can sit alongside the care you already receive.

Can we work together online?

Yes. 1:1 mentoring and Lovelight Circles are available online, and in person where possible.

What does it cost?

Investment depends on the path you choose. We’ll go through options on your free intake call, so you can decide with no pressure.

How do I start?

Book a free intake call. It’s a relaxed conversation about where you are and what you need.
