The JOY Connection Map

Three connections, restored together, bring you home to your light. This is the pattern that carried me through, and the one I now walk with every client.

JHeaven

Connecting to Heaven’s power through prayer, stillness and trust.

OOthers

Connecting to others in healthy, honest, loving relationships.

YYou

Connecting to you: self-care, your interests, healthy habits and compassion.

Meet Ashley

I learned to walk again. Then I learned to fly.

After 2020 I spent three years unable to walk. In that stillness, the patterns of the JOY Connection Map came to me, grounded in my training in neurobehavioral healing.

Today I’m a mother of six, a model, artist, actress and humanitarian. Most of all, I’m a woman who knows what it is to lose your light, and how it feels when it comes back.
