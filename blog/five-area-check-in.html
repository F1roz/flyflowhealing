A five-area check-in for your week

By Ashley RoseNovember 5, 20264 min read

This is a simple practice I share with clients. It takes about ten minutes, a quiet space and something to write with. It isn’t a test, and there are no wrong answers. It’s a way of noticing where your light is shining and where it needs care.

How to do it

Find a moment when you won’t be interrupted. Take three slow breaths. Then move through the five areas below, giving each one a score from 1 (dim) to 5 (bright), and answering its question in a sentence or two.

1. Mental

Ask: What thought has been playing on repeat this week, and is it kind?

2. Emotional

Ask: What feeling have I been pushing away, and what would it say if I let it speak?

3. Spiritual

Ask: When did I last feel connected to Heaven, and what was I doing?

4. Physical

Ask: What is my body asking for right now: rest, movement, water, nourishment?

5. Relational

Ask: Who left me feeling lighter this week, and who left me feeling heavier?

Every area touches the others. Care for one, and you’ll often see another begin to brighten.

What to do with your answers

Look at your lowest score. Choose one small, kind action for that area this week. Keep it small enough that you could do it on a hard day. Next week, check in again and notice what changed.

This check-in is for reflection and is not a substitute for medical or mental health care. If an area feels heavy for a long time, please reach out to a professional you trust. And if you’d like to explore your results with me, book a discovery call.
